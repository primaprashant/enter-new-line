# Privacy Policy

EnterNewLine collects no data.

- **No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking.** The extension makes no network
  requests of any kind.
- **No external services.** Nothing leaves your browser.
- **Local storage only.** Your settings (enabled default sites, usage
  counters) live in your browser's `storage.sync` area. If you are signed into
  your browser, your browser may sync that data across your own devices using
  your browser vendor's sync infrastructure (Google Sync for Chrome, Firefox
  Sync for Firefox). EnterNewLine itself never sees that traffic.
- **Permissions used.**
  - `storage` — to remember your settings and stats.
  - Host permissions for the default sites (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini,
    Perplexity, NotebookLM) — required to run the keypress handler there.
- **Source.** EnterNewLine is open source under the MIT License. The full
  source is available at <https://github.com/primaprashant/enter-new-line>.

If this policy ever changes, the change will appear in this file in the
repository's git history. There is no separate user-facing notification
channel because the extension does not collect contact information.
